Frequently asked questions

Are the online courses on Toppertube really free?

Yes. Every lesson, course tool, note feature and certificate on Toppertube is free, and you do not need an account or a credit card.

Can I get a certificate for a free online course?

Yes. Create a course from a YouTube playlist, a long video or your own list of lessons, finish every lesson, and Toppertube issues a certificate with a code anyone can check on the verify page. It confirms that you completed the course on Toppertube. It is not an accredited degree or an exam-board qualification.

How do I turn a YouTube playlist into a course?

Open the Courses page, choose "From a playlist", paste the playlist link and press Create course. Each video becomes a lesson, grouped into modules you can rename and reorder.

Can I make a course from one long video?

Yes. If the video has chapters in its description, each chapter becomes a lesson with its own name. If it has none, the video is split into equal lessons that you can rename.
